Edits in piece.html
-------------------
D1 (delivery)   Removed the two preconnect links and the Google Fonts
                stylesheet (source lines 7-9). In their place, @font-face
                rules load the same families and styles (Michroma; Instrument
                Serif normal and italic; weight 400; font-display swap) from
                fonts/ by relative URL.
D2 (delivery)   Added <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"> to
                <head>. The canonical route frames this file; the file itself
                is not indexed.
D3 (delivery)   Pointed the ten <script src> tags (source lines 129-138) at
                vendor/three-r128/<package path>, in the same order.
D4 (delivery)   After the renderer's canvas is added to #stage (source line
                165), added the line:
                  renderer.domElement.tabIndex=-1;
                Reason: framed in the site's iframe, a click on the piece did
                not move focus into the frame, so its keyboard controls did
                nothing after a click until the visitor tabbed in. three.js
                r128 OrbitControls cancels pointerdown's default focus and
                calls the canvas's focus(), which does nothing on a canvas
                without a tabindex. With tabindex -1 that call focuses the
                canvas; the canvas stays out of the Tab order. Approved by the
                owner on 2026-11-07.
B1 (behaviour)  Source line 909, before:
                  var spin=dt*Pnow;
                after:
                  var spin=reduceMotion?0:dt*Pnow;
                Reason: under prefers-reduced-motion the piece already freezes
                its RGB cycling, I/O pulse, RAM shader, bobbing and final
                camera sway, but the five fan rotors kept turning after
                power-on, including while paused and after the timeline ended.
                With reduced motion off, the expression is the original
                dt*Pnow. Approved by the owner on 2026-10-22.
V1 (visual)     The piece's ground takes the hue of the site's Darkfield dark
                theme. Each colour keeps its luminance; only the blue-violet
                tint moves to the green-black of --field-deep (#030403) and
                --field (#070b08). Before -> after:
                  --bg (source lines 12, 22, 24)    #080913 -> #030403
                  bar gradient (line 64)             rgba(5,6,13,a) -> rgba(3,4,3,a)
                  label backing (line 36)            rgba(7,8,17,.6) -> rgba(7,11,8,.6)
                  clear colour (line 169)            0x04050a -> 0x030403
                  fog colour (line 171)              0x090a14 -> 0x090b09
                  background sphere (line 672), linear RGB:
                    top  (0.005,0.0055,0.011)  -> (0.00468,0.00623,0.00468)
                    hor  (0.015,0.016,0.032)   -> (0.0137,0.0182,0.0137)
                    bot  (0.004,0.004,0.008)   -> (0.00347,0.00462,0.00347)
                The sphere's brightening with the lights, the violet power-on
                band, the floor glow, the lights and all materials are
                unchanged. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-08.
