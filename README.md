# Kareem Sasa

My corner of the web: systems to tinker with, scenes made from code, and ideas that take a little longer to explain. There's room here for both a traffic jam and a quiet day in a meadow.

## Explore

- **[Simulations](https://kareemsasa.dev/simulations)** — Change the conditions. See what happens to the traffic, the orbits, or the next generation.
- **[Animations](https://kareemsasa.dev/procedural-animations)** — Watch a mechanical movement assemble itself, or descend through the ocean into the dark.
- **[Writing](https://kareemsasa.dev/writing)** — Why do bottlenecks move? What should a machine have to explain? Essays and field notes that follow those questions.
- **[Projects](https://kareemsasa.dev/projects)** — Follow a sound through Linux, a question through a research pipeline, or a system back through its history.
